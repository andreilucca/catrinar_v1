Who am I

A purposeful journey to self-improvement

For students, for teachers, and for myself. Always learning, always moving.

I am a Romanian-born International Baccalaureate (IB) educator with more than 15 years of international experience across the Middle East and South-East Asia. I enjoy taking students and teachers on a purposeful journey to self-improvement, building meaningful and engaging learning environments while integrating the latest technology.

My approach

Conceptual understanding through the ACT model

What is worth learning in Physical Education? I plan learning around the ACT model created by Julie Stern, Kayla Duncan and Trevor Aleo, so understanding travels beyond the gym and into real life.

A

Acquire

Support students to organize pieces of information into sets of ideas: knowing the concepts, their attributes, definitions and perspectives.

C

Connect

Make connections between concepts and build understanding of conceptual relationships, using thinking skills and applying them beyond the school context.

T

Transfer

Transfer new understanding into real-life situations, from similar to dissimilar contexts, ending in students taking their own meaningful action.
